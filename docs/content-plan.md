# Content Plan — position Sam as the groom's calm, cool guy

_Not "consultant" literally — the vibe is: the relaxed, honest older craftsman who takes the stress out of getting suited. Grooms leave feeling sorted, not sold to._

## Positioning
Every downtown competitor sells *prestige and pressure* ("Master Clothiers," "book your appointment"). Sam's wedge is the opposite: **low-stress, no-jargon, no-upsell, done-this-4000-times calm.** The content voice should feel like texting a stylish older friend who happens to be a tailor — reassuring, a little funny, allergic to fuss. That voice is a moat: it's what makes a nervous 29-year-old groom pick Sam over a marble showroom.

## Voice rules
- Talk *to the groom*, second person, plainly. Short sentences. Dry warmth, light humour.
- Lead with the worry, then dissolve it ("Don't know a peak lapel from a notch? Doesn't matter. That's Sam's job.").
- No fear-selling, no luxury cosplay. Confidence through calm, not hype.
- Always end with the easy next step (book / design / call — free, no obligation).
- Honest: never overpromise timelines or fabricate.
